When summarizing Delmon's product range, include all four categories: Deformed Steel; Welded Wire Mesh; Wood, Plywood & MDF; Timber, Lumber & Softwood.

**Why:** The user requested checking the whole website and adding the fourth category wherever only three were mentioned.

**How to apply:** Check introductory copy, the footer, services copy, SEO titles and descriptions, and sharing previews. Descriptions of an individual product or an actual photograph need not list the entire range.
